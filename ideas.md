# TECH SEVA 1.0 Design Direction

## Chosen direction
**Circuit Commons** — an institutional hackathon interface that feels like a well-engineered control room rather than a generic admin dashboard. Probability: 0.08.

## Design movement
SRM-inspired institutional precision meets an airy futuristic hardware lab. White/off-white surfaces carry technical navy ink, red actions, and theme-specific spectrum accents. The feeling is confident, bright, civic-minded, and built for teams who want to make something real.

## Core principles
Use generous whitespace, strong alignment, readable technical data, restrained glassmorphism, thin borders, and visual rhythm from circuit traces, brackets, slot labels, and small status lights. Every motion should clarify hierarchy or make a meaningful action feel tactile.

## Color philosophy
Deep navy (#0b1d38) anchors content and navigation. SRM red (#d5293f) signals action and institutional energy. Agriculture is leaf green, smart cities is electric blue, automation is safety orange, and healthcare is magenta-coral. Surfaces stay warm white so accent colors feel intentional rather than noisy.

## Layout paradigm
Use an editorial dashboard: a wide hero with an asymmetrical right-side system panel, compact metadata rails, four metric cards, theme cards with clear accent rails, and dense but breathable tables. The fixed right-bottom navigation is the persistent compass. The spin page switches to a centered stage that gives the wheel room to breathe.

## Signature elements
A flat TECH SEVA monogram with an interlocking circuit-loop shape, micro-labels in mono, subtle dot-grid and circuit-line backgrounds, thin red rules, “live” signal dots, and cards with a small top-left label/number. Buttons use uppercase labels with a short arrow glyph and tactile hover lift.

## Interaction philosophy
Make status and eligibility obvious. Search/filter controls should update with no page reload. Locked allocations should feel final but not punitive. Empty states should explain the next step. Keep touch targets generous and preserve the wheel’s selected problem as the source of truth from the backend.

## Animation
GSAP entrance sequences use short staggered fades/slides, number counting, row reveals, and soft hover motion. The wheel gets the richest motion: rapid multi-turn easing into the exact server-selected segment, followed by a brief success celebration. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Typography system
Use a modern sans stack for readable product copy, with a condensed/monospace treatment for IDs, scores, slots, and labels. Headings are compact and declarative; supporting copy stays warm and concise.

## Brand essence and voice
TECH SEVA is inventive, civic, practical, and optimistic. Voice: direct, encouraging, professional, and lightly technical. Prefer “Lock a problem” and “Ready to build?” over generic enterprise jargon.

## Wordmark/logo
Use a full-bleed square icon with a strong looped circuit symbol in navy/red on warm white, plus a transparent horizontal lockup in the header. The icon remains legible at favicon size.

## Signature brand color
SRM red (#d5293f), paired with deep navy (#0b1d38) and warm white (#f7f9fb).
